GB-2 / GB98 Builder Knowledge Base

Internal reference for license planning, GB-2 exam preparation, shop-drawing review, and field language. ABQ ADU currently carries GB98; builders also need to understand GB-2 residential construction concepts.
License PathCurrent license context: GB98. Builder training path: GB-2 exam knowledge for residential building, code language, framing assemblies, and job-site responsibility.
Stick Framing OpeningsFor conventional framing, window and door openings need a rough opening (RO) and framing vocabulary: king studs, jack studs, cripple studs, header, sill plate, sill foam, zip sill tape when threshold wood is removed, and fireblock where required.
PUR / SIP Panel ApproachPUR panels and SIP panel systems are a different build method: openings are pre-cut and prepared from panel shop drawings. Muras can help provide these panels, and materials/labor COGS may be dramatically reduced versus stick framing. The software should route panelized jobs to panel layout, cut list, connection details, substitutions, delivery, and installation checks rather than assuming field-built jack/king/header framing.
Opening TypesTrack special openings separately: transom window, clerestory window, large sliders, full glass panels, and any opening over the engineering threshold. Some components are interchangeable or substitutable; substitutions must preserve code, structure, weather sealing, and inspection approval.
